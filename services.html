Common questions

Before you send the first enquiry

Which markets do you currently supply?

We ship from Hong Kong to distributors, workshops and retail partners across Asia-Pacific, and handle cross-border documentation as part of the service.

Can you help identify the right part?

Yes. Send a vehicle reference or an existing part number and our automobile information consultation team will confirm fitment before we quote.

What are your minimum order requirements?

Minimums depend on category and packaging. We will confirm them clearly in the quotation so there are no surprises at invoice stage.

How does the management app help our team?

Orders, inventory, supply chain tracking, consulting dashboards, investment planning and wholesale management are all handled in the app, on both Google Play and the Apple App Store.

How do you handle customs and documentation?

Import and export of goods and technology is managed in-house, including commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin and customs coordination.

Can we open a business account?

Yes. We work with importers, workshops and distributors on account terms, with pricing tiers, credit limits and a named account contact managed through the app.
