Privacy Policy

How Chongyi Automobile Co., Limited collects, uses, shares and protects personal data through this website and through the Chongyi mobile management app published on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Chongyi Automobile Co., Limited ("Chongyi", "we", "us" or "our") handles personal data when you visit chongyiqc.com (the "Website"), when you download or use the Chongyi mobile management application distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play (the "App"), and when you contact us by email, telephone or through the enquiry forms on this Website.

This Policy applies to our customers, prospective customers, business contacts, distributors, workshop partners, suppliers, visitors to the Website, and users of the App. It also applies to advertising and analytics providers acting on our behalf, as further described in Section 9.

Our registered business scope, which contextualises the services to which this Policy relates, is: Wholesale and retail of auto parts and car supplies; automobile information consultation; business consultation; import and export of goods and technology; supply chain management; general trade (excluding regulated businesses such as finance, insurance and lending).

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal data in the following categories.

2.1 Account and contact data

  • Full name, company name, business email address, telephone number and correspondence details.
  • Account credentials for App users, including a hashed password and device registration record.
  • Order, quotation and enquiry history, including parts lists, vehicle references, quantities, delivery addresses and pricing tiers.
  • Billing, invoicing and customs documentation data, where required to fulfil an order.

2.2 Device and technical data

  • IP address, device model, operating system, application version, browser type and language.
  • Crash logs, diagnostic data and performance statistics used to keep the App and Website stable.
  • Session identifiers, referrer information and timestamps of interaction with the Website or App.

2.3 Usage data

  • Pages visited, features used, search terms, and actions taken within the App, such as viewed part references, saved quotations and tracked shipments.
  • Advertising interaction data, including impressions, ad requests, clicks and video playback events.

2.4 Optional location information

  • If you enable location services, the App may collect your approximate location to help confirm delivery eligibility, calculate lead times and show nearby partners. We do not collect precise location in the background, and we do not use location data for advertising purposes.

2.5 Advertising identifiers

  • The Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and the Google Advertising ID (AAID), together with related advertising identifiers assigned by advertising networks, as permitted by your device settings.
  • Data collected through advertising SDKs, as described in Section 9.

2.6 Communications

  • Emails, messages and support tickets you send to us, and the replies we send back.
Sensitive data: We do not intentionally collect special category data (such as health, biometric or political opinion data), and we ask that you do not submit it through the enquiry forms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  1. To perform our contract with you, including processing orders, quotations, shipments, invoices and customs documentation.
  2. To provide the App features covering real-time orders, inventory, supply chain tracking, consulting dashboards, investment planning, and wholesale and retail management.
  3. To respond to enquiries, quotations and support requests, and to keep a record of business correspondence.
  4. To provide automobile information consultation and business consultation, including fitment confirmation and market or category guidance.
  5. To manage supply chain operations, including sourcing, consolidation, warehouse allocation, quality inspection and delivery scheduling.
  6. To maintain the security, integrity and availability of the Website and the App, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  7. To personalise content and advertising, subject always to your consent and device settings as described in Section 9.
  8. To measure the performance of our marketing and advertising campaigns.
  9. To comply with legal, regulatory and contractual obligations, including export control, sanctions screening, tax and customs record-keeping.
  10. To send service announcements or business updates where you have consented or where they are necessary for the operation of an account.

We do not sell personal data for money. We do share certain data with advertising partners as described in Section 9, and where that constitutes a "sale", "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under applicable law, we do so only with a valid opt-in consent where that is required.

9.2 Personalised and non-personalised advertising

  • Personalised (behavioural) advertising uses your advertising identifier, app activity and inferred interests to select ads. Where required by law, this is presented only after you give consent through our consent platform, which also honours the Global Privacy Control signal described in Section 15.
  • Non-personalised (contextual) advertising is served using only the content of the page or app screen you are viewing, without building a profile of you. Where you have opted out of personalised advertising, we limit the advertising identifiers shared with our partners and ask them to serve contextual advertising only.
  • Where the App Tracking Transparency framework applies (Apple devices, iOS 14 and later), we request your permission before tracking. If you decline, we serve contextual advertising and do not access the IDFA for advertising purposes.
  • On Android, the AAID is reset if you clear "Delete advertising ID" in your device settings. We then receive a limited, non-unique identifier and cannot re-associate it with you.

10. Advertising Formats and Your Controls

The App and the Website may present the following advertising formats:

  • App open (开屏) ads — displayed when the App is launched. Typically skippable after a short countdown and not rendered on every launch.
  • Rewarded video ads — opt-in video that you choose to watch in exchange for a stated benefit, such as free delivery on a quotation or extended shipping tracking. You may decline at any time without losing functionality.
  • Interstitial ads — full-screen ads shown at natural break points, such as after completing an order or closing a tracking screen. They are not shown during active data entry.
  • Banner ads — small static or animated units placed within the interface and clearly distinguishable from functional content.

You control these formats through your device settings and through the in-app privacy options: limiting ad tracking in your operating system, resetting your advertising ID, revoking consent in the consent platform, or uninstalling the App. We also honour a universal opt-out from Google Ads (GPC) and the corresponding limit-ad-tracking controls on iOS and Android.

13. Children's Privacy and Age Policy

The Website and the App are directed at businesses and business users. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction, which may be 16 in certain European countries).

  • We comply with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

15. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Where a browser or device sends a "Do Not Track" (DNT) or "Global Privacy Control" (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to limit data collection and to serve non-personalised advertising where applicable. We do not use DNT or GPC to serve behaviourally targeted advertising, and we do not sell personal information in response to these signals. Because there is no agreed standard for DNT across all browsers, we apply a uniform, privacy-protective interpretation.
