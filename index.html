Mobile management app

The entire operation, in your pocket

The Chongyi management app is a first-class part of how we trade — not an add-on. It carries the same business scope that our desk operates on: real-time orders, inventory, supply chain tracking, consulting dashboards, investment planning and wholesale and retail management.

Real-time orders Place, amend and track orders as they move from confirmation to dispatch.
Inventory control Live stock positions across categories, warehouses and reserved allocations.
Supply chain tracking Milestone-level visibility from supplier dispatch to your receiving dock.
Consulting dashboard Market notes, fitment guidance and business consultation output in one view.
Investment planning Scenario planning for stock investment, category expansion and project budgets.
Wholesale & retail management Pricing tiers, credit limits, account teams and document history per customer.
