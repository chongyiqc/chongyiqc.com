Our culture

Built by people who have handled the wrong part — and never forgot it

Chongyi Automobile Co., Limited grew out of the trade floor, not a boardroom. The habits we keep — check the fitment twice, call the customer before the delay, sign the paperwork — are the reason our buyers stay.

What drives us

Mission, vision, values

Three statements we can be held to.

01

Mission

To deliver auto parts and car supplies that arrive correct, documented and on the date we promised — and to make wholesale and retail in the parts trade something a business can plan around.

02

Vision

To be the Hong Kong counterparty that distributors and workshops in the region call first — known for fitment accuracy, transparent supply chains and technology that removes paperwork.

03

Values

Accuracy over speed, clarity over silence, documentation over memory, and long-term relationships over one-off transactions. These are the four checks we run every order through.

Milestones

How the desk grew

Each step added a capability our customers had been asking for.

Founding

The company is established in Hong Kong

Chongyi Automobile Co., Limited opens in Tsim Sha Tsui with a focused remit: auto parts and car supplies wholesale and retail for local workshops and distributors.

Expansion

Import and export of goods and technology

Cross-border trading is formalised in the registered scope, with in-house customs coordination and documentation so clients deal with one party from purchase to delivery.

Capability

Automobile information consultation is added

A dedicated consultation function is built around part cross-reference and fitment verification, reducing wrong-order returns and shortening the quotation cycle.

Integration

Supply chain management goes end to end

Sourcing, consolidation, warehouse allocation, inspection and last-mile scheduling are run as one managed chain with milestone-level visibility for the customer.

Advisory

Business consultation & investment planning

Beyond moving goods, the team starts advising distributors on category strategy, pricing, market entry and stock investment planning.

Digital

The management app ships to Google Play and the App Store

Real-time orders, inventory, supply chain tracking, consulting dashboards, investment planning and wholesale and retail management move into one mobile product for the whole business scope.

Team spirit

A desk of specialists, a company of optimists

Our people are buyers, technicians, logisticians and analysts who happen to enjoy getting a hard consignment through customs on time. We keep the office friendly because the work is stressful enough.

  • Four eyes before shipping Every consignment is checked by a second colleague before it leaves.
  • Proactive updates If a shipment slips, you hear it from us before you have to ask.
  • Tools over paperwork Technology absorbs the chasing so people can do the judgement work.
Compliance & responsibility

Global compliance, done quietly

We operate across jurisdictions with different rules on data, advertising and trade. Our default is to meet the strictest applicable standard, keep the records, and tell customers what they need to know.

The website and the management app are covered by our published Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, which set out how personal data is handled, which advertising partners are involved and what rights you hold in your jurisdiction.
